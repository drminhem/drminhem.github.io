Types of cancer

Consultations cover all adult solid cancers, including:

  • Breast cancer
  • Lung cancer
  • Colorectal cancer
  • Stomach cancer
  • Esophageal cancer
  • Liver cancer
  • Bile duct cancer
  • Pancreatic cancer
  • Kidney cancer
  • Bladder cancer
  • Prostate cancer
  • Testicular cancer
  • Ovarian cancer
  • Uterine cancer
  • Cervical cancer
  • Head and neck cancers
  • Thyroid cancer
  • Skin cancer and melanoma
  • Brain tumors
  • Sarcomas
  • Neuro­endocrine tumors
  • Cancer of unknown primary

You can request a consultation even if your diagnosis is not listed.

What to expect at your consultation

Review the diagnosis

We review your medical history, pathology, imaging, and relevant blood tests together.

Discuss the options

Understand the available options and how treatment planning or monitoring relates to your case.

Agree on next steps

Discuss your questions, follow-up, and what further information may be needed.

What to bring

  • Pathology or biopsy reports, if available.
  • Imaging and scan reports, recent blood tests, and previous treatment records.
  • Your current medications and the questions you want to discuss.

Contact us to arrange the appointment, and bring your medical reports to the consultation.
