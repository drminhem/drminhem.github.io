Common questions

What happens at the first appointment?

We review your history and reports together, explain your diagnosis in plain language, and agree on the next steps. You leave with a clear plan — not more confusion.

Can you review my case and explain my options?

Yes. Bring your pathology, scans, and reports to your consultation. We review your diagnosis and discuss the available options together.

What do you treat?

Solid tumors—including breast, lung, colorectal and other digestive cancers, prostate, kidney, and other solid tumors—along with lymphoma, leukemia, other blood cancers, anemia, platelet and clotting disorders, and abnormal blood counts.

Will I need chemotherapy?

Not always. Many patients are treated with targeted therapy, immunotherapy, tablets, radiation, surgery, or simply close monitoring. We choose what truly fits your case.

Can a family member be with me?

Absolutely. Having a family member with you — in person or by phone — often makes understanding and decisions easier.

How soon can I be seen?

Appointments at both clinics are arranged in advance, with flexible scheduling. Contact us by WhatsApp or phone to confirm a suitable time before visiting.
