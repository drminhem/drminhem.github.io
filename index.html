Practical questions

Can I come for a second opinion?

Yes. Bring the reports and treatment recommendations you already have so they can be reviewed together.

Will I need chemotherapy?

Not always. Options depend on the cancer type, stage, test results, and your health. We discuss whether chemotherapy or another approach is appropriate for your case.

Can a family member come with me?

Yes. You may bring a family member to help discuss your questions and next steps.

How do I confirm my appointment?

Send an appointment request by WhatsApp or call. Both clinics offer flexible scheduling by prior appointment; confirm the time before visiting.

This website provides information and does not replace emergency care. In an emergency, go to the nearest emergency department.
