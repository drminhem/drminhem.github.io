Understanding your breast cancer results

Your biopsy and scans help show the type of breast cancer and how far it has spread. Hormone receptor results and other tumor features also help guide the treatment discussion.

How I can help

A treatment plan tailored to your cancer, test results, overall health and preferences, informed by current evidence.

What do my biopsy results mean?

Review the type and grade, estrogen and progesterone receptors (ER/PR), and HER2 protein results, alongside imaging and lymph-node findings.

Which options fit my breast cancer?

Discuss the roles of surgery, radiation and medicines. Hormone treatment, HER2-targeted treatment and immunotherapy apply to selected subtypes and situations, depending on the results and stage.

Why this treatment plan?

Clarify treatment order, possible side effects and any missing information. A second opinion considers current evidence, your health and preferences, and can help you discuss the plan with your treating team.

Reports to bring, if available

Bring the reports you already have. This list does not mean every test is needed.

  • Existing biopsy or surgical pathology report, including ER, PR and HER2 results.
  • Mammogram, ultrasound or breast MRI reports and images already obtained.
  • Any existing lymph-node reports, staging scans and treatment summaries.

Please also bring your medication list, previous treatment records, and questions.

Questions about your breast cancer plan

  • How do my hormone receptor and HER2 results affect the options?
  • What is the reason for the proposed order of surgery and other treatments?
  • What information is still needed to assess the proposed plan?
